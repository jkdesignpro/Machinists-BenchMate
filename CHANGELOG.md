# Changelog

All notable changes to Machinists BenchMate. Versions follow semver.

## [Unreleased]
### Added (CNC guide, nose radius compensation, formula library)
- **Tool nose radius compensation** (Turning): Z and X shifts for a chamfer at any angle (dz = r (1 - tan(θ/2)), dx = r (1 - tan((90° - θ)/2))), the imaginary-tip path for any chamfer, taper or arc with the end on a face, a diameter or free, any tip direction 0 to 9, the error left with no compensation, a to-scale drawing, and the same element programmed with G41/G42. Each tip number has its own drawing; the screen notes that Haas and MachMotion charts differ.
- `benchmate-core` module `tnrc` (tip vectors, closed forms, general line and arc solver) with tests against the formulas printed by Kentech/TWI Press and an Industry Arena thread, the nose-circle tangency, OD, ID, centre-tip, convex and concave cases; WebAssembly exports `tnrc_*`.
- New **Guides** group. **CNC knowledge guide**: ISO 841 axes and the right-hand rule, machine, work and tool coordinates, work offsets (G54-G59 and extended), tool length, radius and wear offsets, block and word format, modal groups, safe start line, comments, subprograms, climb and conventional milling, chip formation, seven tool wear types with causes and remedies (Mitsubishi Materials and Seco), turning and milling troubleshooting tables, workholding and a short shop safety page. Own drawings; every statement carries its source keys and is marked "to be verified" automatically when it has fewer than two independent public sources.
- **Formula library**: 130 formulas from 74 tools, searchable and filterable by group, each with variables and units, source keys and a button that opens the tool. Built from a registry (`app/formulas.js`); the smoke test checks that every entry names a real tool and every calculator has an entry.
### Added (fasteners and machine elements)
- New **Fasteners** group. **Bolt grades**: ISO 898-1 classes 4.6 to 12.9, SAE J429 grades 2, 5 and 8, ASTM A574 socket head cap screws; proof, yield and tensile minimums with publisher counts (inch mode in ksi) and our own head-mark drawings.
- **Bolt torque and preload** (VDI 2230-1:2015, cited): tightening torque MA = F (0.16 P + 0.58 d2 muG + DKm/2 muK), permitted assembly preload from yield utilisation nu, preload from percent of proof load, or a known preload. Thread and head friction are required inputs with Bossard's friction classes shown beside them; bearing diameter from the across-flats and clearance tables or typed; results show d2, d0, stress area, DKm, torque with the share of pitch, thread and head friction, nut factor K and the assembly equivalent stress. Where publishers disagree on the strength used (ISO 8.8 proof), all values are listed.
- **Socket head cap screws** (ISO 4762, ASME B18.3): head diameter and height, hex key, clearance hole and counterbore (MSC and MISUMI side by side) with a drawing. **Clearance holes**: ISO 273 fine/medium/coarse M1.6 to M48 and inch close/normal/free, drill names converted with the drill chart. **Spanner and hex key sizes**: ISO 272, old DIN and JIS across flats, inch hex, and a lookup from a spanner size to the threads it fits. **T-slots** (ISO 299): slot, head space and depth min-max, bolt size, pitches, section drawing.
- New **Machine elements** group. **Keys and keyways**: metric (DIN 6885-1 / ISO 773) and inch (ASME B17.1) parallel keys from the shaft diameter, keyseat and hub depths and check dimensions (inch with the chordal height). **O-ring glands**: AS568 -001 to -475, piston, rod and face glands, squeeze, stretch (with cross-section reduction), gland fill against Global O-Ring and Apple Rubber guidance, and a groove suggested from your targets. **Bearing rating life**: L10 and L10h from C and P (ISO 281), the C needed for a life, and P = X Fr + Y Fa.
- `benchmate-core` module `fasteners` (stress areas ISO and Unified, VDI 2230 torque and permitted preload, nut factor, O-ring squeeze/stretch/fill and design helpers, bearing life, keyseat dimensions) with tests against MSC tensile loads, the marek VDI 2230 torque table, the Global O-Ring squeeze table and the ZKL L10 tables; WebAssembly exports.
- Evidence file `data/refdata/fasteners-evidence.json` (31 source entries). `scripts/build_refdata.py` builds every fastener table with exact-value consensus (13.5 and 14 are not merged), converts inch drill names, and stops if a row has no publisher.
### Added (GD&T round 3, desktop microphone, letter addresses)
- **GD&T guide:** composite profile section (upper row locates, lower row floats with orientation-only datums; same datums in the same order); datum letters (no I, O, Q; AA, AB after Z); statistical tolerance (ST) explained with its SPC note; projected zone and tangent plane wording sharpened; RFS circled S history by edition (required 1982, legacy option 1994, gone from 2009); ISO 5459:2024 edition 3 (2024-10) and ISO 22081:2021 as the replacement of ISO 2768-2:1989; position on planes (ISO yes, ASME no); two-point gauges and odd lobing. Each now has two independent public sources (new: PTC Creo help, GLS 2012 edition chart, Hexagon PC-DMIS help, SIS, BSI, Quality Magazine, Modern Machine Shop, PMPA). Still flagged "to be verified": resultant condition at LMC, position without datums on coaxial features, ISO invocation principle, MMC on profile in Y14.5-2018, the 2018 fastener appendix letter, V-block angle rule, spherical-zone and tangent-plane usage notes. Details in `docs/gdt.md`.
- **Feature control frame builder:** I, O, Q datum letters are now invalid (confirmed rule); projected zone, statistical tolerance and tangent plane notes no longer flagged.
- **G and M codes:** "Letter addresses" table A to Z, with a NIST RS274NGC column and a Haas mill column, each from its own document (parity BB73).
- **Desktop microphone** for the chatter finder: the app grants microphone requests from its own page (camera and screen capture denied), switches on WebKitGTK's media stream setting on Linux, and ships `desktop/Info.plist` (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription) and `desktop/Entitlements.plist` (audio-input entitlement for the hardened runtime). The release workflow re-signs macOS builds with the same entitlements and checks them. Verified on Linux (built app, virtual display, PulseAudio test tone found at 1233.4 Hz); macOS and Windows are set up but not tested here (see `docs/release.md`).
### Fixed
- Grinding wheel marking and grit conversion screens failed to load (unescaped quote in `app/grinding.js`); `scripts/build-web.sh` now syntax-checks every UI script.
- Chatter finder listed the side lobes of one tone as separate peaks (a single 1234 Hz tone showed 1226, 1219 Hz and so on). Peaks inside the Hann main lobe (2 bins) of a stronger peak, or not 15 dB above its side-lobe envelope, are now dropped, as are peaks more than 40 dB below the largest; frequencies are still refined by parabolic interpolation. New tests with single tones (on and off bin, 44.1 and 48 kHz, drifting), two far and two close tones.
### Added (calculators batch 3: small mill and lathe, chatter, grinding)
- **Milling:** radial chip thinning (feed for a target chip, engagement angle, with a drawing), corner and boss engagement (law of cosines, feed reduction into an inside corner), ball-nose cusp height and step-over (exact circle form), ball-nose effective diameter with tool tilt (speed and feed corrected), runout and chip load (per-tooth chip with dial-indicator TIR, feed that keeps the worst tooth at the target).
- **Turning:** lathe feed (mm/min and mm/rev), multi-pass roughing time with a per-pass table, boring bar deflection (steel, carbide, heavy metal or your own modulus; optional coolant bore; overhang guidance from Seco, Kennametal and Cutwel shown side by side), C-axis feed (degrees per minute, feed along the circumference, inverse time F for G93).
- New **Sawing** group: slitting saw (speed, feed, cut time) and bandsaw (chip per tooth from down-feed, blade speed and TPI; cut time from a cutting rate).
- **Spot and centre drill depth** for a chamfer diameter, and the reverse.
- New **Chatter and vibration** group: **chatter finder**. Type a chatter frequency, or record a few seconds with the microphone: the spectrum is drawn, peaks that are spindle or tooth-passing harmonics are marked, and stable spindle speeds (lobe table, nearest speeds above and below) are listed and checked against the active machine. Sound is analysed in memory only; plain messages when the microphone is refused, missing or not allowed on an insecure page.
- New **Grinding** group: wheel speed (m/s, sfpm, rpm for a speed; checked against the maximum speed you read off the wheel, which is never defaulted), cylindrical grinding (equivalent diameter, contact length, speed ratio, specific removal rate, equivalent chip thickness, traverse and plunge time), surface grinding time, **grit size conversion** (ANSI, FEPA F, FEPA P; each publisher's value with confirmed / differs / single-source badges, nearest equivalents) and a **wheel marking decoder** (for example 38A60-I8VBE or WA 60 K 7 V: abrasive, grit, grade, structure, bond, maker's record, with disagreements between sources shown).
- Evidence file `data/refdata/grinding-evidence.json` (eight publishers with URL and access date) built into `data/refdata.json` by `scripts/build_refdata.py`.
- Our own SVG drawings for engagement, cusp, ball-nose tilt, boring bar, spot drill, equivalent diameter and surface grinding.
- `benchmate-core` modules `millturn`, `chatter` (radix-2 FFT, Hann window, peak interpolation, harmonic detection) and `grinding`, with tests against published worked examples, hand examples, a direct DFT and brute-force scans. WebAssembly exports for each, plus a sample buffer for the microphone spectrum (`chat_buf_ptr`, `chat_add`, `chat_peaks`).
- A tool can now draw a diagram under its results (`diagram(v, sys)` in `app/tools.js`).

### Added (G-code cycle generators)
- New **G-code cycles** group, seven tools. Every program carries a warning header (generated by BenchMate, check against your control manual, prove out in single block), G20/G21, and a decimal point in every coordinate and feed word.
- Drilling and tapping cycles: G81, G82, G83, G73 and G84 (rigid or floating; F = pitch x rpm) for a bolt circle, a grid (zigzag order) or pasted coordinates; G98/G99 return; hole list CSV download.
- Thread milling program: internal or external, right or left hand, climb or conventional, 90 degree arc-in and arc-out tangent to the thread, optional G41/G42 with a D register, radial and spring passes, single- or multi-form tools; path radius, tool-centre feed, helix angle and 3D helix feed.
- Lathe threading: Fanuc-style two-block G76 (P mmrraa, Q and P in least input increments), Haas one-block G76 (P1/P3), or one G92 block per pass with flank infeed; infeed table for constant volume (from the first cut, d x sqrt n, or over n passes), constant depth or custom depths, minimum cut, finish allowance and spring passes.
- Lathe taper program (external or internal, roughing along the cone, finish pass, optional nose radius compensation), groove and cut-off with CSS (G50 clamp, G96, G97 at the end, cut time with CSS), keyway broaching on a lathe or mill, G71/G70 rough and finish from a list of profile points (Fanuc-style two-block or Haas one-block).
- Per-control dialect table: generic ISO 6983 / Fanuc-style and Haas. Mitsubishi M70/M80, Siemens 840D ISO mode, Heidenhain ISO and Okuma OSP are listed as "not yet supported" and cannot be selected. Sources per cycle and dialect in `docs/cycles.md`.
- 2D backplot (XY for the mill, Z against X for the lathe) and an estimated cycle time (feed, rapid, dwell) with the rapid rate from the screen or the active machine profile; highest rpm and feed checked against the active machine.
- Machine profiles: optional rapid traverse rate.
- `benchmate-core` modules `cycles` (infeed tables, thread depths, thread-mill path radius and centre feed, helix feed, CSS rpm and plunge time, tapping feed) and `gcode` (dialects, generators, moves and time), with tests against the ISCAR infeed example, Harvey Performance and Travers thread-mill feeds, CSS hand example and numerical integration, and 21 golden-output programs. WebAssembly exports `cyc_run`, `cyc_text`, `cyc_move`, `cyc_time`, `cyc_info`, `cyc_table`, `cyc_supported`, `infeed`, `pattern_grid`, `holes_csv` and the formula functions.
